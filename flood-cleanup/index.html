A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Lehi Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Lehi and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Lehi

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. A trained cleanup team knows how to set containment, wear PPE (personal protective equipment) correctly and sanitize by protocol, which protects your household from hepatitis, E. coli and other diseases carried in water.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Wherever flooding is likely, get appliances and electrical equipment up above floor level
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
