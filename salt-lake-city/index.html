Common Water Damage Issues in Salt Lake City

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. These are the ones Salt Lake City property owners report to us again and again.

Water heaters: Over the years, mineral deposits settle in a tank and corrode the metal underneath. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. A split tank does not stop at one tankful, because the inlet keeps feeding it until the valve is shut. When the unit lives in an attic or interior closet, the water reaches carpets and ceilings far from it.

Roofs: One strong storm is enough to peel back shingles and push water beneath the roof covering. Leaks also start around chimneys, vent pipes and skylights where the sealant has dried out. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. On Salt Lake City roof leak calls, our crew removes saturated insulation, sets drying equipment and can cover the opening until a roofer arrives.

Freezing: Depending on the season, a hard overnight freeze can burst lines that were fine the day before. Outdoor spigots, sprinkler feeds and pipes in crawl spaces or along outside walls are hit first. A house left empty or a wing kept unheated faces the greatest risk. If a faucet only trickles on a freezing morning, shut off the water and call before the pipe thaws.

Backups: Gurgling sounds and more than one slow fixture at once suggest the main drain is partly blocked. When the clog becomes complete, every flush and every load of laundry comes back out downstairs. Since the waste is hazardous, we extract it and then sanitize concrete, tile and other hard surfaces. Stay out of the affected rooms and avoid running water until the line is cleared.

Seepage: A wet line along the bottom of a foundation wall is a sign that water is building up in the soil next to it. Short downspouts, soil that slopes toward the house and blocked footing drains are frequent causes. White chalky deposits, peeling masonry paint and a persistent damp smell are early warnings. We remove any standing water in Salt Lake City homes, dry the walls and slab, and explain what likely caused it.
